Strategy: ONE bot, ONE locked market at a time — and on that market the AI re-reads four families every cycle over the last 100 ticks and trades whichever one is ready, one contract per accepted cycle:
• EVEN/ODD — the Synchronization desk's read: the 3 or 4 highest digits all RED bars (≥11.5%), all on one parity, the other parity holding no RED digit of its own, or as the second route in a coverage that clears the Danger gate (σ). Measured payout 1.82× ⇒ 54.95% break-even against a ~50% coin flip.
• RISE/FALL — the 5-model ensemble on the same window; it must agree on the direction with at least the RISE/FALL min conf (default 70%). Measured payout ≈1.80× ⇒ 55.56% break-even.
• OVER 2 ⇄ UNDER 6 — Bot 9's recovery pair on this desk: the cycle starts at OVER 2 (digits 3–9, ~70% coverage, ≈1.34× ⇒ 74.63% break-even) and flips to UNDER 6 (digits 0–5, ~60% coverage, ≈1.54× ⇒ 64.94% break-even) after every loss; either side enters on a clean RED-digit setup or on coverage above break-even. Any win resets the side to OVER 2 and the stake to base.
Families are ranked by expected value, and a RED-digit setup outranks a bare coverage edge. Every one of the four is negative-expectancy at its quoted payout — OVER 2 ≈ −0.06, UNDER 6 ≈ −0.08, EVEN/ODD ≈ −0.09, RISE/FALL ≈ −0.10 per 1.00 staked at its base rate — so most cycles are refused and waiting is the normal state, not a fault.
The market only moves when YOU press TRADE AI NOW (or START after a press): the AI scans every volatility market once, out loud, with the robot scan, and locks this bot onto the best market that has a family ready. While running the bot never switches markets on its own — if no family is ready on the locked market it holds and waits for the ticks to move. Martingale risk: the shared ×2.20 ladder recovers the first loss outright at 1.82× and needs ≈×2.85 to win a deep run back in full; the OVER 2 leg at ≈1.34× cannot recover a loss by staking alone at all. Deeper runs are cut off by the steps/cap. Keep the base stake small and the stop loss on. No setting, no family and no ladder turns these payouts into positive expectancy.
