Strategy — cover the top-ranked digits, stop on the first hit: every cycle the bot ranks all ten digits of the last 50 ticks of the current market by share plus momentum (the last 25 ticks against the first 25, so a digit that is waking up outranks one fading — ties go to the more recently seen digit), then buys DIGITMATCH on the top "Digits per cycle" digits at once (default 3). All legs are bought in the same second, so they all ride the same exit tick. Your rule is wired in: the first cycle that contains a hit ENDS THE RUN and the popup reads RUN BANKED ON THE FIRST HIT — take profit and stop loss still take precedence, and STOP always works. The hot-share band now gates the leader of each cycle (not one locked digit): no legs are bought while the ranked leader sits under the min hot share (it can rank first on momentum while another digit still holds a taller raw share — a flat market hands every digit ~10%), and the cycle is refused when the leader is over-heated at or above the max hot share — across 10,000 real ticks a hottest-of-50 leader past 22% repeated on the next tick only 9.3% of the time (6.9% past 24%), i.e. a streak running out rather than a trend. After a missed cycle every leg's stake is multiplied by the martingale factor until a hit lands or the per-leg stake cap forces a reset to base. Measured payout: a 1-tick match returns ≈6.66× the stake, so K legs stake K and need joint coverage of K ÷ 6.66 — 15.02% at 1 digit, 30.03% at 2, 45.05% at 3, 60.06% at 4 — and recovery needs a multiplier of at least 1 + K ÷ (6.66 − K) (≈×1.82 at 3 legs). Read this honestly: on this market's last 1,005 ticks (955 rolling windows) the top-3 set held 43.2% of its own window but covered the next tick's digit only 31.3% of the time against the 45.05% it needs — and the same ≈−30% of everything staked came out at every leg count (1 digit 11.6% vs 15.02% needed, 2 digits 20.4% vs 30.03%, 4 digits 42.4% vs 60.06%). Ranking by momentum beat raw share by about one percentage point — inside the noise. Covering more digits buys hits, not an edge: you get a winning cycle far more often and a smaller win each time, and the run ends as soon as one lands. Keep the base stake small and the stop loss real.
